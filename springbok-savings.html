💰 Monthly Deposit
Each member contributes their agreed monthly amount. Deposits cannot exceed the agreed monthly contribution. Each cycle, one member receives the full combined pot — the total of all contributions. Members rotate in order of join date.
🚨 Emergency Withdrawal
Max withdrawal = amount deposited this month. Member must settle amount owed PLUS next month's contribution. Repaying in the same month clears the next month's obligation.

🏆 Issue Stokvel Payout
The stokvel pot is the combined total of all members' monthly contributions. It is paid to the next member in the rotation queue. The amount is auto-filled.
